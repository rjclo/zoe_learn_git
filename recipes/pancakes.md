# Fluffy Pancakes 🥞

## Ingredients

- 1 cup flour
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 egg
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 tablespoon melted butter

## Steps

1. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
2. Whisk the egg, milk, and butter in another bowl.
3. Combine wet and dry, stir until just mixed.
4. Cook on a hot pan until golden on both sides.
5. Serve warm with your favorite topping.

> Tip: Try adding blueberries!
